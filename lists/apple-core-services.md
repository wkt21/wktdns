# Apple Core Services (Allowlist)

Essential Apple domains that should remain reachable for iOS/macOS functionality, updates, iCloud, and device services.

## iCloud & Configuration
- gateway.icloud.com
- mesu.apple.com
- ocsp.apple.com
- ocsp2.apple.com
- configuration.apple.com
- xp.apple.com
- bag.itunes.apple.com
- gsp57-ssl-background.ls.apple.com
- get-bx.g.aaplimg.com
- www.apple.com

## Push, CloudKit, Maps, Time
- push.apple.com
- api.apple-cloudkit.com
- api.apple.com
- weather-data.apple.com
- maps.apple.com
- time.apple.com
- device-services.apple.com
- itunes.apple.com

## Notes
These domains are required for proper operation of Apple devices. They are explicitly allowlisted to prevent accidental breakage when using aggressive denylists.
