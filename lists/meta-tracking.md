# Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Tracking Domains

Domains associated with Meta platforms used for tracking, graph API, CDN, and social features.

## Instagram
- lookaside.instagram.com
- www.instagram.com
- instagram.c10r.facebook.com

## Facebook Graph & Connect
- connect.facebook.net
- graph.facebook.com
- b-graph.facebook.com

## CDN
- static.xx.fbcdn.net

## Notes
These domains power Facebook/Instagram tracking pixels, login, and content delivery. Blocking them improves privacy but will break Meta login and social embeds on many sites.
