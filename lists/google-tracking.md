# Google Tracking Domains

Domains associated with Google tracking, advertising, analytics, and telemetry that are blocked in the WKT-DNS denylist.

- accounts.google.com
- google.com
- www.google.com
- ogs.google.com
- clients1.google.com
- clients2.google.com
- clients3.google.com
- clients6.google.com
- ogads-pa.clients6.google.com
- jnn-pa.googleapis.com

## Notes
Blocking these domains significantly reduces Google tracking and ad delivery across the web. Some Google services (Search, Maps, YouTube) may experience partial degradation depending on allowlist exceptions.
