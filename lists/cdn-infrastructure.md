# CDN Infrastructure (Allowlist)

Major CDN and certificate authority domains that underpin modern web delivery. Blocking these can break large portions of the internet.

## CDNs
- cloudflare.com
- cdn.cloudflare.com
- akamai.com
- akamaiedge.net
- fastly.net
- fastly-edge.com
- apple-dns.net

## Certificate Authorities
- digicert.com
- letsencrypt.org
- verisign.com

## Notes
These are foundational internet infrastructure. Keep them allowlisted unless you have a highly specialized offline or air-gapped use case.
