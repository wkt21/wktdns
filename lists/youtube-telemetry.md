# YouTube Telemetry Domains

Domains used by YouTube for video delivery, telemetry, accounts, and related Google services that are blocked or restricted in WKT-DNS.

## YouTube Core
- www.youtube.com
- m.youtube.com
- accounts.youtube.com
- youtubei.googleapis.com
- youtube.googleapis.com

## Video Delivery & Thumbnails
- googlevideo.com
- redirector.googlevideo.com
- i.ytimg.com
- s.ytimg.com
- yt3.ggpht.com
- ytimg.l.google.com
- ytimg.com

## Notes
Blocking YouTube-related domains will break or severely limit YouTube functionality. These are included in the denylist for maximum privacy environments. Users who need YouTube should add exceptions via the allowlist or NextDNS custom rules.
