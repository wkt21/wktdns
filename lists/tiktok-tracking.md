# TikTok Tracking Domains

Domains used by TikTok for API calls, logging, and app/web telemetry.

## Core TikTok
- www.tiktok.com
- m.tiktok.com
- log.tiktokv.com
- api.tiktokv.com
- api16-normal-c-useast1a.tiktokv.com

## Notes
TikTok is known for aggressive telemetry. Blocking these domains prevents most TikTok tracking and may break the TikTok web/app experience.
